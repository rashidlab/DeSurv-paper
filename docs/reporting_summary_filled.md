## Statistics
For all statistical analyses, confirm the following are reported:

- **n (sample size) for each experiment/group:** Yes. Training (pooled TCGA + CPTAC)
  n = 273 (139 deaths). Combined external validation n = 570 unique patients (388
  deaths); per-cohort sizes after QC in Supplementary Table (cohorts). Simulations:
  100 replicates per scenario.
- **A description of all covariates tested:** Yes. Adjusted analyses include the PurIST
  (tumor-intrinsic) and DeCAF (stromal) classifier calls as covariates, with dataset as
  a stratifying factor.
- **A description of any assumptions or corrections (e.g. Cox PH):** Yes. Cox
  proportional-hazards assumption assessed by Schoenfeld residuals (cox.zph) on the
  pooled validation linear predictor: PH is not supported (chi-square 14.7, df 1,
  P = 1.3e-4); the effect attenuates over follow-up. The reported hazard ratio is
  therefore stated as a time-averaged summary and complemented by a proportional-
  hazards-free proper score (integrated Brier score / index of prediction accuracy).
- **Test statistics, degrees of freedom, exact P values:** Yes. Exact P values reported
  throughout (or P < 0.001); no "P < 0.05" placeholders. Effect estimates reported with
  95% confidence intervals.
- **Central tendency and variation:** Yes (medians/IQR for simulation distributions;
  point estimates with 95% CIs for hazard ratios and C-indices; +/-1 SE ribbons for CV
  curves).

### Life-sciences study design

- **Sample size — how determined:** No a priori sample-size calculation. This is a
  secondary analysis of existing cohorts; all available samples from each public /
  controlled-access cohort meeting the pre-specified eligibility criteria were included
  (see Data exclusions). Cohort sizes are fixed by the source studies. For simulations,
  100 replicates per scenario were used, sufficient for stable estimation of the median
  C-index and gene-program recovery and their spread.
- **Data exclusions:** Yes, on pre-established eligibility criteria only. Included:
  primary PDAC tumors with a measured bulk expression profile and non-missing overall-
  survival time and event. Complete-case analysis; classifier-adjusted analyses
  restricted to samples with available PurIST/DeCAF calls. For combined analyses, the
  PACA-AU array/RNA-seq platform overlap was de-duplicated to one record per patient
  (RNA-seq retained, array duplicate excluded), per the DeCAF preprocessing rule.
  Pre-QC and post-QC counts are given in Methods and the Supplementary Information. No
  post-hoc, outcome-dependent exclusions.
- **Replication:** External replication across five validation datasets spanning four
  independent patient cohorts; simulations repeated over 100 independent replicates; the
  final model was initialized from a consensus over 100 random initializations (empirical
  optimization stability: 100/100 converged, monotone objective). All analyses are
  fully reproducible from the deposited code. All replication attempts are reported.
- **Randomization:** Not applicable to group allocation (observational secondary
  analysis; no experimental intervention or arms assigned by the investigators).
  Cross-validation folds were randomized within dataset-stratified groups using fixed
  seeds.
- **Blinding:** Not applicable. Program discovery, scoring, and model fitting are fully
  algorithmic with no human-in-the-loop scoring or subjective assessment; investigators
  did not assign samples to groups.

### Human research participants
- **Population characteristics:** Human pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) patients.
  Training and the five bulk-transcriptomic validation cohorts are de-identified primary,
  treatment-naive, non-metastatic tumors. Translational cohorts are treated PDAC
  (metastatic and locally advanced). Per-cohort characteristics summarized in the
  Supplementary Information; demographic stratification is limited by inconsistent
  annotation across source studies (stated in Methods, "Ethics, sex and gender, and ancestry").
- **Recruitment:** All participants were recruited by the original studies/consortia
  (TCGA, CPTAC, ICGC/PACA-AU, Moffitt, Dijk, Puleo; and the treated trials). We performed
  no recruitment; selection into this analysis was by the eligibility criteria above.
  Potential selection bias inherent to the source cohorts is acknowledged.
- **Ethics oversight:** Secondary analysis of de-identified human data. Public/controlled-
  access transcriptomic cohorts required no new IRB approval for this analysis; controlled-
  access data (PACA-AU via EGA) were used under the repository's access terms. The
  restricted clinical-trial cohorts (COMPASS/O'Kane, Linehan) were obtained
  from the study investigators under data-use agreements, subject to the governing trial
  consents and institutional approvals held by those studies. Appropriate ethics approval
  and informed consent were obtained by each originating study, as reported in their
  primary publications.

### Clinical data
- **Study protocol / registration:** The treated-cohort data derive from previously
  reported clinical trials and cohorts (COMPASS/O'Kane; the Linehan FOLFIRINOX +/- CCR2-inhibitor cohort). Trial design and
  registration are in the respective primary publications, which are cited. We conducted
  a retrospective secondary analysis; no new trial was run.
- **Data collection / outcomes:** Overall survival (and progression-free survival where
  available) as recorded by the source studies. Outcomes were defined by the original
  studies; we used them as provided without re-adjudication.

## Software and code

  data were obtained as processed matrices from the sources listed in Data availability.

  optimization for hyperparameter selection implemented within DeSurv), archived at

## Data availability
A full data availability statement is in the manuscript. Summary: TCGA-PAAD and CPTAC-3
(NCI Genomic Data Commons); Dijk (ArrayExpress E-MTAB-6830); Puleo (ArrayExpress
E-MTAB-6134); Moffitt (GEO GSE71729); PACA-AU array (GEO GSE36924) and RNA-seq (EGA
EGAS00001000154, controlled access). Molecular classifications (PurIST, DeCAF) for
validation cohorts were compiled for the DeCAF study. The Linehan cohort and COMPASS/O'Kane are restricted-access clinical-trial datasets available from
the study investigators under a data-use agreement; derived per-cohort summary statistics
are provided in the reproducibility repository (results/treated_cohort_stats.rds) so every
reported value can be verified without redistributing restricted data.
